## Opportunities

`mini highland cow` is the clearest snippet problem. The filtered page is near position 2 and receives 217,531 impressions in this export for 230 clicks. The same export’s named query, `mini highland cow experience`, has a 24.09% CTR. The title stored in Rank Math for the open page is `The Original Mini Highland Cow Experience | Udderly Ridiculous`. The draft does not have an SEO title yet. A title that says it is a visit, and a description that says where and what happens, is the change to test. Do not repeat the query.

Sale queries are already efficient. `mini highland cow for sale`, `mini highland cow for sale ontario` (526 clicks, 45.38% CTR, position 1.12), and `highland cow for sale` (535 clicks, position 5.33) should keep pointing at Animals for Sale and the individual listings. Do not aim the experience page at “for sale”.

`adopt a highland cow` has 300 sitewide clicks, 3,808 impressions, and position 9.23. The adoption hub has 791 clicks. Clara’s old URL alone has 1,049 clicks. Publish the adoption drafts and redirect `adopt-clara` to `/adopt-an-animal/clara` before cutover.

`alpaca picnic` has 230 sitewide clicks at position 10.18. Alpaca Lunch Picnic is the matching preserve URL, and its draft has no SEO fields yet. The stored Rank Math description is the candidate to review.

`cow cuddling near me` has 245 sitewide clicks at position 6.29. That intent is split across cow visits. Do not create a new page for it until the owner chooses which visit should answer it.
